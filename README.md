**It's free.** Download, run, done. No account, no payment.

**It runs on your PC.** Works offline, and your voice never leaves your computer.

**It never cuts you off.** Your words appear as you speak, and it stops only when you stop it.

**Stop, and it's typed.** Chat box, document or coding tool — right where your cursor was.

**One key is enough.** Hold `G` to start, hold it again to stop. A quick tap still types g.

**Your mouse becomes a keyboard.** Bind the hotkey to a spare button. Logitech gestures can also handle Enter, paste and copy.

**Start and stop with your own word.** Enroll it in Settings → My voice, then say it to start or stop.

## Get started

1. Download with the button above and run it.
2. Follow the first-run guide: say hello, then check your microphone.
3. Choose “On my PC (free, works offline)” or “With a Gemini API key.”
4. For local dictation, download a speech model once (about 250 MB or 1.1 GB). For Gemini, enter your key.
5. Try it with the guide: hold `G`, speak, then hold it again to paste at your cursor.

## Want it more accurate?

You can **try Gemini for 10 seconds without a key** (three times a day) from the first-run guide or Settings.

Add a Gemini API key in Settings to transcribe with Google's speech recognition. Keys are free at [Google AI Studio](https://aistudio.google.com/apikey); usage follows Google's free-tier limits and pricing. In this mode your voice is sent to Google.

<details>
<summary>Common questions</summary>

**Windows blocks it.** The app is unsigned, so Windows warns the first time. Choose **More info → Run anyway**.

**Where do my text and voice go?** Transcripts are saved as Markdown in the history folder you choose (default: `Documents\mulbit`). Audio is not saved by default.

**Can I use a key other than G?** Yes, in Settings. You can also set a hotkey such as `Alt+Shift+V` and assign it to a mouse button in your mouse software (Logitech etc.).

**Which Windows?** Windows 10 and 11.
